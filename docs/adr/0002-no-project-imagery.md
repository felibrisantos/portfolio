# No project screenshots and no per-project pages

Four of the five projects are client work whose interfaces cannot be published:
the assets would need a clearance that is not coming. The decision is to ship
without them and without per-project detail routes, rather than to wait or to
substitute. The project cards are the full depth of the project content.

## Consequences

The site's verifiable evidence is narrow on purpose: the paper's DOI, the one
publicly reachable product, and this repository. Everything else is a claim a
reader is asked to take on trust. That is the accepted ceiling of the site.

Per-project pages were considered separately and rejected on their own merits:
with no imagery and no public link behind four of five projects, a longer page
makes a claim longer, not more verifiable.

Read the absence as a constraint that was accepted, not as work that was
dropped. In particular, do not close the gap with placeholder imagery, stock
mockups or generated screens — the site's whole argument rests on not claiming
more than it can show.
